📖 How to Play

Har happens. We tap. We win.
1
Someone in real life says "Har?" — the moment you hear it, grab your phone.
2
Tap your own cat as fast as you can. First person to tap wins that round: +1 Har, and $1 goes into the shared Har Fund.
3
Only the first tap counts. Everyone else's tap that round does nothing — no penalty, just no point.
4
For 3 seconds after a Har is caught, the Har Generator 👹 can tap other players' cats to attack them: −1 Har, no money added.
5
If you tap your own cat before the Har Generator gets to it, you're 🛡️ Protected — the attack does nothing.
6
Your score can never go below 0, no matter how many times you're attacked.
7
🔥 Streaks = catching Hars back-to-back. Getting attacked or losing a round to someone else resets your streak, not your total.
8
Every 5 Hars, your cat gets visibly chunkier. Everyone can see whose cat is the fattest.
9
Everything resets fresh every Monday — new week, new leaderboard, new chonk.
10
The Har Fund is real $ the group uses for lunch/tea — it only grows from successful catches, never from attacks.
